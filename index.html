Built by Ranjan Sahoo,
a CSE undergrad at VSSUT.

I built this after watching classmates — my own cohort — lose days refreshing spreadsheets during counseling. There wasn't a tool that felt honest about what it was: a reference, not a guarantee.

Cutoffs are pulled from publicly available OJEE counseling records going back five admissions cycles. The buffer logic for reservations, SGS, and gender follows the standard weightage applied in past rounds.
